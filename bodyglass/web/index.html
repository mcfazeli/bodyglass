About BodyGlass

BodyGlass turns your own MRI into a 3D model you can explore. Everything is computed on this computer; your scans are never uploaded.

Educational use only. It is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis or treatment decisions. Shapes are computer estimates; ask your doctor about your results.

Built with open tools: SynthStrip (brain outline), FastSurfer (brain structures), TotalSegmentator (neck), three.js (3D view).

Export for 3D printing

The model is made solid and watertight on this computer and saved life size in millimetres (1 unit = 1 mm). Printing tips: a whole brain is about 14 × 17 × 14 cm, so most home printers need it scaled down (try 50–70 %) or split. Use supports, 0.2 mm layers and 10–15 % infill; small parts such as the hippocampus print well at 100–200 %. Touching parts are merged into one solid piece. This is an educational model, not for planning treatment.
